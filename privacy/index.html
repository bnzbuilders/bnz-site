Who this covers

This policy covers the public website at buildwithbnz.com, the staff workspace at bnz-app.vercel.app (later app.buildwithbnz.com), and the BuildWithBNZ iPhone app (bundle com.bnzbuilders.opssuite).

What we collect

Contact forms on the website go to bnzbuilders1@gmail.com so we can answer you.

Staff sign-in on the web workspace uses Clerk for email and session. We do not put Clerk secret keys in the app binary or in git.

The iPhone app stores staff email, job labels, RFQ drafts, and site photos you take on the device unless you later connect a company cloud account. Camera access is used only when you tap Take site photo.

What we do not collect

We do not sell lists. We do not publish bonding figures, job-cost dollars, or private owner correspondence. Precise contacts from your address book and payment cards are not collected by this public website or by the v1 iPhone app.

Agents

Sara, Emma, and Taylor help draft RFQs and checklists. They are not a licensed estimate, bid, or legal advice. Bid totals stay UNKNOWN until verified documents exist.
