Privacy

How we handle your information.

BuildWithBNZ is operated by BNZ Builders Inc., 424 Rockaway Turnpike, Cedarhurst, NY 11516.

Last updated: September 2026

What this page covers

This policy explains what we collect through this public website and the BuildWithBNZ workspace, and how we use it. It does not create rights beyond those required by applicable law.

Information you send us

  • Contact and quote forms are delivered to us by email so we can respond. We use the details you provide (name, company, email, phone, and message) only to reply and to help set up your account.
  • We do not sell or rent contact information.

Your workspace data

  • The BuildWithBNZ workspace stores your company's operating records for authorized users of your account.
  • Work you bring in — projects, estimates, documents, and correspondence — is used to provide the service to your company and is not shared with other companies.
  • We do not publish private job costs, bonding figures, or owner correspondence on this website.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout and are handled by Stripe — this website does not collect or store full card numbers.

Cookies and analytics

The public website uses only what is needed to function. Your signed-in workspace may use a session cookie to keep you logged in.
